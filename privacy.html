Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Strangely True Stats is a private tool used by a single person, the owner of the Strangely True History channel, on their own computer. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's sign-in screen, Strangely True Stats reads, read-only:

  • YouTube Data API: the owner's own channel and its videos (titles, publish dates, view, like and comment counts).
  • YouTube Analytics API: the owner's own channel analytics (views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained and lost).

With the same consent through TikTok's sign-in screen, it reads the owner's own TikTok account, read-only: profile statistics (followers, likes, video count) and the owner's public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

It does not upload, change or delete anything, and it does not access any other account or user.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner how their own videos perform. It is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Google and TikTok user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.

Strangely True Stats' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage, retention and deletion

Data is kept in local files on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
